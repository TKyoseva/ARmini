Group 10 · I2C-0 to the PCA9685 and the MLX90642-D45 (10a–10d)

AR-Mini, pool/lake run 31.10.2026 · whole group 10 · right board rails as on your board: red + INNER, blue − OUTER

  1. S1 OFF, USB unplugged.
  2. 10a green (SDA): R-c16 (row of EXT2-16 = GPIO48) → PCA9685 SDA and D45 SDA. One wire to a splitter or two wires from row 16 (c16 and e16 are the same strip).
  3. 10b teal (SCL): R-d17 (row of EXT2-17 = GPIO47) → PCA9685 SCL and D45 SCL.
  4. 10c red: PCA9685 VCC and D45 3V3 → right + rail (inner, 3.3 V from 3a).
  5. 10d black: PCA9685 GND and D45 GND → right − rail (outer).
  6. PCA9685 header V+ and OE stay empty; servo power comes from the green terminal (group 15).
  7. Do not swap SDA and SCL: rows 16 and 17 are next to each other. Check with USB in: PCA VCC = 3.2–3.4 V, power LED on; the serial monitor shows the PCA9685 at 0x40.
