Where the cables come in (logic box 150 × 110, board centred: 8 mm to each long wall, 23 mm to bow / stern)
  • L1 port wall ≈ 60–100 mm from the bow — one bundle from the power box: VBAT sense → J1 (bow-port), ESC signal → J2, leak comb → J9b, USB power → DevKit USB-C (90° adapter, points along the port margin), V+ pair → PCA9685 terminal (bow).
  • L2 starboard knock-out ≈ 30 mm — lift servos → PCA servo field, XIAO 5 V → J13, MLX90640 → J7.
  • L3 starboard ≈ 73 mm — rudder servo → J3 (column 30, rows 26–28).
  • L4 starboard ≈ 116 mm — MLX90642-D45 → J6; the logic-box leak comb → J9 at the stern edge.
  • KILL button on the lid above the stern-port corner → J10 (C + KILL); RUN stays unused with a latching button.
Rules while soldering
  • Headers first: plug both female 1×22 onto the DevKit, put the sandwich into rows 14 / 24 (pin 1 at column 30), solder 2 corner pins of each, pull the DevKit, solder the rest. Same for the PCA9685 (columns 4 / 26) and the BNO055 (columns 18 / 25).
  • The EXP-14 is double-sided with plated holes: a bare bridge is soldered to every pad it crosses, so never run one through a hole that belongs to another net (the drawing never does).
  • U6 socket notch to PORT (pin 1 = column 4, row 30). 3A / 4A / 3OE / 4OE go to +5V (unused channels stay off).
  • Nothing taller than 2 mm on top under the DevKit (rows 15–23) or under the modules; all routing there is on the bottom.
  • Before the modules go in: meter beep test of every net in the table, and NO beep between 3V3, +5V, VBAT and GND.
