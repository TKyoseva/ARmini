Row 9a · +5 V bus from the DevKit (EXT1-21)

AR-Mini, pool/lake run 31.10.2026 · only this connection, everything else as in FINAL 2

  1. S1 OFF, USB unplugged.
  2. One orange wire from L-g21 (same row as EXT1 pin 21 = +5V, the pin next to GND at row 22). Do not use row 22: that is GND.
  3. The wire goes off the board to a 3-way splitter (Wago / terminal block / 3-way Dupont).
  4. From the splitter, three orange wires: R-j23 (74HCT125 pin 14 VCC, row 6h) · MG90S red (8c) · XIAO 5V (13a).
  5. Meter before the ESC is plugged in: USB in, R-j23 to the blue − rail = 4.6–5.0 V (≈ 4.65 V is normal, the DevKit Schottky eats ~0.3 V).

Known risk (FINAL 2 notes): if the DevKit resets when the rudder moves, the MG90S draws too much through EXT1-21. Then stop and tell me; the fix is a separate 5 V feed for the MG90S red only.
