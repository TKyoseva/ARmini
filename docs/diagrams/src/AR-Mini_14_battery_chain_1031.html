  3. Outputs: F1 20 A → thruster red (14d) · F2 15 A → QIQIAZI IN + (14e) · F3 empty · F4 5 A → MZHOU IN + and the thin VBAT wire (14g + 5d).
  4. Black: LiPo −, thruster black, MZHOU −, QIQIAZI − all into the GND bus (14i); one black wire from the bus to the right − rail (14j).
  5. Continuity check: + to − on the fuse block outputs → no beep. Then fuses in, battery in, S1 ON, MZHOU USB-C plug = 5.0–5.2 V before it goes into the DevKit (14h).
