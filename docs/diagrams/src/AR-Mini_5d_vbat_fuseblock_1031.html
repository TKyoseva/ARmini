Row 5d · VBAT sense wire from fuse F4 to the breadboard

AR-Mini, pool/lake run 31.10.2026 · only this connection, everything else as in FINAL 2

  1. KILL on, S1 OFF, battery unplugged.
  2. F4 output terminal takes two wires under the same screw: the thick red to MZHOU IN + and the thin red sense wire. Twist the two ends together (or crimp both into one ferrule/spade) so the screw grips both.
  3. Run the thin red wire to the left breadboard and push it into L-e10 (row 10, column e). Nothing else goes in row 10 except the top leg of the 100 kΩ (L-c10).
  4. Check the divider is already in: 100 kΩ L-c4 ↔ L-c10, 30 kΩ L-a4 → L − rail, blue jumper L-e4 → L-f4 (GPIO4).
  5. Meter before power: S1 ON, then measure row 10 to GND ≈ battery voltage (12.6 V full), row 4 to GND ≈ 2.9 V. If row 4 shows more than 3.3 V, S1 OFF at once: a resistor is wrong.

Why F4: the sense wire is protected by the 5 A fuse, so a short on the thin wire blows F4 instead of melting it. Stop the run at VBAT ≤ 10.5 V (row 4 ≈ 2.4 V).
