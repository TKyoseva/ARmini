Rules while soldering

  • P1 headers: plug both female 1×22 onto the DevKit first, then put the DevKit + headers into P1 and solder 2 corner pins of each header ("sandwich" — SPEC §2). Then all 44.
  • Column 1 of P1 stays empty except the EXT pins: that end is under the antenna. Nothing on top under the DevKit taller than 2 mm; all wiring there is on the bottom.
  • Never bridge a header pin to the hole next to it unless the drawing shows it (e.g. EXT1-19 and R5 are neighbours but different nets).
  • P2: socket notch to the LEFT (pin 1 = 1OE bottom-left). 3A / 4A / 3OE / 4OE go to +5V here (TI: unused inputs to VCC or GND, both fine); outputs 3 and 4 stay off because their OE is HIGH.
  • J2 ESC pin order follows SPEC J2 (S · NC · GND). The thruster's white 3-pin plug is still unmeasured: find the ≈5 V pin (signal) and the 0 V pin first, then build the adapter.
  • Before the DevKit goes in: meter beep test of every net in the table, and NO beep between 3V3, +5V and GND.
