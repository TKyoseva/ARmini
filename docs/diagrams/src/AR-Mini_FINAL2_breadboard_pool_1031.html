AR-Mini · FINAL 2 pool breadboard (31.10.2026) · with the cradle lift

Top view: antenna up, USB-C down. The DevKit-Lipo stands across two BB400 boards (EXT1 on the left board column j, EXT2 on the right board column a, header pin n = row n). Modules sit off-board on cables. Left board: KILL rocker, battery divider, leak probe, I2C-1. Right board: 74HCT125 at +5 V below the DevKit (GPIO1 → 1A, 1Y → thruster yellow; GPIO2 → 2A, 2Y → MG90S orange; 5 V pulses, not inverted; no S8050), I2C-0 → PCA9685. Right column: QIQIAZI 5 V buck on F2 → PCA9685 V+, two TD-8120MG on CH10/CH11 = the cradle lift. No tracks. Firmware env remote_pool only. Blue badges = rows of the wire table below. Supersedes the FINAL sheet of 02:23.

Assembly order

  1. Boards and DevKit: EXT1 in L-j rows 1–22, EXT2 in R-a rows 1–22, antenna up. Rails: groups 1, 2, 3.
  2. Left board: KILL rocker 4 (heat-shrink the three tabs), VBAT divider 5, leak probe 12 (optional today).
  3. Right board: 74HCT125 6 (notch up, row 23), then the thruster input/output 7 and the rudder 8. Measure 4.6–5 V on pin 14 after group 9.
  4. +5V bus 9 from EXT1-21 to the chip, the rudder red and the XIAO 13.
  5. I2C-0 10 to the PCA9685 (and the D45), I2C-1 11 to the MLX90640.
  6. Lift 15: QIQIAZI buck, 5 V check on the cut cable, then V+ / GND into the PCA9685 terminal, then the two TD-8120MG on CH10 / CH11.
  7. Battery chain 14 last, rocker in KILL, S1 OFF: continuity check + ↔ − on every rail and on V+ (no beep), then power on in the order below.

Wire list · tick each row

Power-on order: rocker KILL → S1 ON → 5.0–5.2 V on the MZHOU plug, 4.6–5 V on chip pin 14, 5 V on the PCA9685 V+ terminal → WiFi "AR-Mini", http://192.168.4.1, VBAT ≥ 11.5 V → lift slider at the bottom (50), U deployed → rocker RUN → ARM (the arms may twitch a few degrees) → turn left/right, release → thrust → lift up slowly to 130, down to 50. Stop and lift out at VBAT ≤ 10.5 V.
