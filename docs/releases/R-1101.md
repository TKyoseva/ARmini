# R-1101 · 01.11.2026 · G1

**What it shows:** AR-Mini left the bathtub. On one thruster hung from the beams (beam_leg), steered by a printed rudder, wired from the FINAL 2 sheet into a food box and powered by the 3S LiPo, it was launched from the bank at Pancharevo in rain and fog, drove and turned from the phone and came back to the bank. Before that: the right thruster's fault proven on a third driver, the left track driven from the DevKit, both thermal cameras giving first frames, the whole robot assembled dry with tracks.
