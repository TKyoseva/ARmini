# PCA9685 — datasheet notes for the carrier rev B (U2)

Read 15.10.2026 against `pcb/carrier-revB/SPEC.md` (C032). The PDFs are not in git (FAILS 114): local copies in `build/datasheets/`.

- NXP PCA9685 product data sheet, Rev. 4, 16 April 2015 (52 pp) — https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PCA9685.pdf
- Adafruit "16-Channel PWM Servo Driver" guide (30 pp) — https://learn.adafruit.com/16-channel-pwm-servo-driver — our U2 is an Ardboard clone of the Adafruit rev C design (same schematic, different outline: 58.98 × 25.92, holes 54.26 × 17.36 measured, vs Adafruit 62.2 × 22.4). Always use the measured numbers for the footprint.

## What the carrier relies on — confirmed

| Carrier (SPEC §) | Datasheet | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| U2 VCC = +3V3 (§4) | VDD 2.3–5.5 V (NXP §12, Table 14); the module's 10 k SDA/SCL pull-ups go to VCC (Adafruit p. 7) | OK. VCC must stay 3V3 — at 5 V the module would pull SDA0/SCL0 to 5 V on the ESP32-S3 (same rule as the BNO055 Vin). |
| SDA0/SCL0 pull-ups R3/R4 4.7 k (§3) | Fm+ part; VIL ≤ 0.3·VDD, VIH ≥ 0.7·VDD (Table 14) | OK. 4.7 k ∥ 10 k (U2) ∥ 10 k (U3) ≈ 2.8 k → ~1.2 mA per line at 3.3 V, within the I2C 3 mA sink. |
| KILL: Q2 pulls OE low in RUN; R10 2.2 k to 3V3; the module has 10 k OE→GND (§6) | OE active LOW; OE = 1 → every LEDn = 0 with the default OUTNE = 00 (NXP §7.4, Table 11). OE is a plain input: VIH = 0.7·VDD = 2.31 V at 3.3 V, VIL = 0.99 V (Table 14, "Address inputs; OE input") | OK: KILL = no servo pulses, buzzer off, LEDs off. Margin check: 3.3 × 10/(10+2.2) = 2.70 V, 0.4 V above VIH. Measure OE–GND on the unpowered module: if ≥ 10 k keep 2.2 k; **1 k is the safer fit (3.0 V)**, same footprint, 3.3 mA through Q2 in RUN. |
| Address 0x40, A0–A5 open (§4) | No internal pull-ups on A0–A5 (NXP §7.1.1) — the module pulls them low; All Call 0x70 is ON at power-up (§7.1.2), SWRST general call 0x00 (§7.6) | OK. An I2C scan shows **0x40 and 0x70 from the one module** — not a second device. Nothing else on I2C-0 sits at 0x70 (BNO055 0x28, MLX90642 0x33). |
| Outputs: servos on CH10/11 from the module, CH6 buzzer (Q3, R11 1 k), CH7–9 LEDs (R7–R9 330 Ω) via JP4 (§7) | Totem pole by default (OUTDRV = 1), 25 mA sink / 10 mA source per pin, VOH ≥ 2.3 V at 3.0 V (Table 14); POR state of every output is LOW (§1); **220 Ω in series with every PWM pin on the module** (Adafruit p. 7) | OK. Buzzer base drive (3.3 − 0.7)/(220 + 1000) ≈ 2.1 mA → S8050 saturates for a 30 mA buzzer. LEDs: 330 + 220 = 550 Ω → ~2.4 mA, dim; **100–150 Ω on the carrier** gives 4–5 mA (fit at soldering, no layout change). No servo twitch at boot: outputs are LOW until the firmware writes them. |
| V+ (servo 6 V) only on the module's screw terminal, pin 6 of U2A/U2B unconnected (§4, §10) | V+ is not used by the chip at all; 5–6 V for servos; never tie VCC to V+ (Adafruit p. 7, 10); capacitor slot: n × 100 µF per servo (p. 11) | OK. Two TD-8120MG → **470–1000 µF / 16 V** in the C2 slot (the "10 mm capacitor" in SPEC §4). |
| PWM 50 Hz for the servos | prescale = round(25 MHz / (4096 × f)) − 1 (NXP eq. 1, §7.3.5) → 121 for 50 Hz (50.02 Hz); PRE_SCALE writable only with SLEEP = 1; 500 µs after wake before RESTART (§7.3.1.1) | Firmware. The internal oscillator is "25 MHz typical" with no tolerance given — clones often run ~27 MHz → the pulse width scales with it. **Measure one channel with the UT61E+ Hz function at 50 Hz and set `setOscillatorFrequency(25e6 × f_meas / 50)`** before the C023 `pair` calibration. |
| One frequency for all 16 channels | NXP §1; Adafruit p. 7 | Firmware: at 50 Hz the LEDs and the buzzer are **ON/OFF only** (`setPWM(ch, 4096, 0)` / `(ch, 0, 4096)`), never dimmed — a dimmed LED at 50 Hz flickers. |
| Reset | POR at VDD 1.7–2.0 V; the chip resets only when VDD falls below 0.2 V (§7.5) | Firmware: a brown-out does not reset it → always start with the library `reset()` (SWRST) + MODE1/MODE2/prescale in `setup()`, never assume power-up defaults. |

## Nothing changes on the copper
All of the above are solder-time values (R10, R7–R9, C2 on the module) or firmware. Rev B goes to Aisler as generated (commit `ca3154a`).

## SPEC wording fix (15.10)
§5 J4: the 3.3 V PWM into the BTS7960 buffer comes from **GPIO39–42 (ESP32-S3)**, not from the PCA9685 — §3 is right, §5 said "from the PCA9685".
