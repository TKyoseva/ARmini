How an analysis is written. At card close (or while it runs, as a draft): the challenge we hit → the solutions that exist, with where they come from → what we chose and why → the transferable lesson → is it a seed for a next activity (research · publication · patent · open source · business) → impact 0–3 on three axes, nature & people first, then financial, then scientific → where it would be done once admitted (course or lab) and which grant or competition fits. University, grant and competition lines are suggestions as known in October 2026: verify names, eligibility and dates when applying.
