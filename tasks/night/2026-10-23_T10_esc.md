# T10 · bench `esc` env: both thruster ESCs on LEDC GPIO1 / GPIO2 · C024

**Branch** `dev` (daytime, small) or `night/esc` → PR to `dev`
**Budget** one to two hours

## Goal
The one bench module T3 left out. Drive the two thruster ESCs from the DevKit so C006 (signal level) and C025 (thruster in a bucket) can run today.

## Context
- PINOUT / `pins_devkit.h`: `PIN_ESC_L 1`, `PIN_ESC_R 2` (EXT2 4 / 5), "no bench sketch".
- Thruster listing (C002, not confirmed for this unit): PWM 50 Hz, 1500 µs = stop, 1500 → 2000 forward, 1500 → 1000 reverse; arming not documented.
- C006 is NOT done: nobody knows yet whether the ESC's yellow signal wire idles above 3.3 V. The PROTOCOL must make that measurement step 1, before GPIO1 is ever connected.
- Same contract as the other bench envs (`pca9685`, `bts7960`): CSV at 10 Hz, `?` help, `0` = safe, dead-man, KILL on GPIO14 (low = KILL), `Ramp`, `SoftLimit`, `Deadman`, `Csv` from `lib/benchcore`.

## Change
- `platformio.ini`: `[env:esc]` extends `esp32s3`, `build_src_filter = +<common/> +<esc/>`. Keep CDC_ON_BOOT=1 like `pca9685` (monitor on the **left** USB-C).
- `firmware/bench/src/esc/main.cpp`:
  - LEDC 50 Hz, ≥ 14-bit resolution, one channel per pin. Both outputs at **1500 µs from the first line of `setup()`**.
  - CSV `t_ms,l_us,r_us,kill,armed` at 10 Hz.
  - `arm`: holds 1500 µs on both for 3 s, prints `# ARMED` after. Nothing else moves until armed.
  - `l <us>`, `r <us>`, `both <us>`: target pulse, ramped at 200 µs/s.
  - `diff <thrust> <turn>`: −100..100 each → `l = 1500 + k·(thrust + turn)`, `r = 1500 + k·(thrust − turn)`, clamped, k so 100 = the current soft limit.
  - Soft limit **1350–1650 µs**; `limit wide` widens to 1000–2000 with a `# WARN` line, `limit` alone prints it. Out of range → `ERR LIMIT`, no move.
  - `0`, dead-man (default 2 s, `dm <ms>`) and KILL → both **1500 µs immediately, no ramp**, `armed = 0`.
  - `?` prints help and state.
- Pure maths in `lib/benchcore/EscMath.h` (diff mix + clamp), like `PairMath.h`.

## Tests (host, `test_benchcore`)
- diff 0 0 → 1500 / 1500; diff 100 0 → 1650 / 1650; diff 0 100 → 1650 / 1350; diff 100 100 → clamped 1650 / 1500.
- Clamp at the narrow and the wide limit.
- Ramp 1500 → 1650 at 200 µs/s reaches the target in 0.75 s.

## PROTOCOL (`firmware/bench/src/esc/PROTOCOL.md`)
1. **C006 first, signal NOT connected.** Thruster in a bucket, duct under water, held down. Jesverty 9.0 V, CC 0.10 A, red + / black −. Meter DC V: yellow ↔ black and white ↔ black. Write both numbers.
   - yellow ≤ 3.6 V → connect directly: yellow → GPIO1 (EXT2 4), white → GND.
   - yellow > 3.6 V → **stop, do not connect** (the ESP32-S3 is 3.3 V only) → level shifter (74AHCT125, JP1/JP2 on rev B).
2. Flash `esc`, monitor on the left USB-C; CSV shows 1500 / 1500, kill follows the rocker (C → GPIO14, KILL → GND, 10 k to 3V3).
3. Then C025: Jesverty 12.0 V, CC 3 A, meter in series (10 A jack); `arm`, listen for the beeps, then `l 1550`, `l 1450`, `l 1600`, `l 1650`, 10 s each; current + direction for each.
4. KILL rocker while spinning, on video: prop stops < 0.5 s.
5. Full power only on the LiPo through the inline fuse + 20 A slot, ≤ 5 s, `limit wide`.
- Log with `bench_log.py --card C025`.
